Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026

1. Overview

SpeakStudio ("the App") is a client-side audiobook creation and playback application for Android and Windows 10/11. SpeakStudio operates directly between your device, your own Google Drive storage, and any local or personal cloud notebook session you choose to run. We do not operate centralized backend servers that collect or store your personal files.

2. Google User Data We Access

When you choose to sign in with Google, SpeakStudio requests permission to access:

  • Basic Profile & Email (email, profile): Used only to display which Google account is currently connected inside the SpeakStudio app interface.
  • Google Drive Workspace (MyDrive/SpeakStudio/): Used strictly to read and write your audiobook text manuscripts (.txt), narrator reference voice clips, generation checkpoints, session heartbeat metadata, and finished .m4b audiobooks inside your own Google Drive.

3. How Data Is Stored & Protected

OAuth tokens are stored locally on your own device using platform encryption (Android Keystore / EncryptedSharedPreferences on Android and Windows DPAPI safeStorage on Windows). When you launch a personal cloud notebook session (Google Colab or Kaggle), credentials are used solely within your own authenticated session to sync your MyDrive/SpeakStudio/ folder.

4. Data Sharing & Limited Use Disclosure

SpeakStudio does not sell, rent, share, or transfer Google user data to third parties or advertising networks, and does not use Google user data to train external AI models. SpeakStudio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Disconnecting & Deleting Your Data

You may click Log Out inside SpeakStudio Settings at any time to erase stored OAuth tokens from your device, revoke SpeakStudio's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or delete the MyDrive/SpeakStudio/ folder directly in your Google Drive.
