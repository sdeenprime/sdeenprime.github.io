Bookspeaker

Bookspeaker is a simple audiobook player for Android and Windows. It helps you listen to your audiobooks, keeps your place in every book, and syncs your library, bookmarks, and listening position across your devices using your own Google Drive.

What it does

  • Plays audiobooks from files on your device.
  • Remembers where you stopped in every book.
  • Keeps your bookmarks safe.
  • Syncs your library between your phone and computer with Google Drive.
