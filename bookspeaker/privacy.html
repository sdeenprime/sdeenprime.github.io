Bookspeaker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

What Bookspeaker does with your data

Bookspeaker is an audiobook player. It only handles the information needed to play your books and keep your place in them. We do not sell your data, and we do not show ads.

Google Sign-In

When you sign in, Bookspeaker uses Google Sign-In to confirm your identity. We receive your basic account information (such as your email address) only to let you sign in and to connect your devices.

Google Drive sync

If you turn on sync, Bookspeaker stores your library details, bookmarks, and listening positions in a private folder inside your own Google Drive (the app-data folder that only Bookspeaker can see). This lets your phone and computer share the same library. Bookspeaker never reads, shares, or stores your other Drive files.

On-device storage

Your books, bookmarks, and listening positions are also stored on your own device so the app works offline. This data stays on your device unless you choose to sync it.

Limited Use disclosure

Bookspeaker's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We only use Google user data to provide and improve the sync and sign-in features described above, and for no other purpose.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.
  • We do not share your data with third parties, except as needed to provide Google Sign-In and Google Drive sync.

Your choices

You can sign out and disconnect Google Drive at any time from the app's settings. You can also remove Bookspeaker's data from your Google Drive at any time.
