SpeakStudio

SpeakStudio converts plain-text (.txt) books and manuscripts into chaptered .m4b audiobooks with synchronized sentence transcripts across Android, Windows, and user-controlled GPU runtimes.

Key Capabilities

  • Multi-Engine Synthesis: Create chaptered M4B audiobooks with Pocket, Chatterbox, Qwen3, and VibeVoice neural speech engines.
  • User-Owned Google Drive Workspace: Stores your manuscripts, reference voice clips, resume checkpoints, and finished audiobooks directly inside your own MyDrive/SpeakStudio/ folder.
  • Local & Cloud Flexibility: Run completely offline on Windows PC hardware or connect to your own Kaggle / Google Colab GPU notebook sessions, with seamless chunk-level resume across devices.
  • Synchronized Playback: Built-in M4B audiobook player with embedded BSJ1 sentence-level transcript highlighting.

How Google Account Access Is Used

When you sign in with Google, SpeakStudio uses Google OAuth 2.0 solely to manage your personal MyDrive/SpeakStudio/ audiobook workspace (books, narrator voice clips, checkpoints, and completed audiobooks) so your library stays synced between your Android phone, Windows PC, and personal cloud notebook runtime. SpeakStudio does not run external servers that collect, store, or sell your Google Drive data.
