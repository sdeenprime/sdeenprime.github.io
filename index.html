Key Studio Capabilities

  • Automatic Manuscript Structuring: Detects chapters, word counts, and optimal synthesis chunks automatically from any .txt book.
  • Expressive Narration Controls: Fine-tune Expressiveness, Steadiness, and Pacing with instant live passage previews before generating a full book.
  • Local & Cloud GPU Freedom: Run completely offline on Windows hardware (NVIDIA CUDA or CPU) or launch automated Kaggle & Google Colab T4 GPU sessions in one tap.
  • Cross-Device Checkpoint Resume: Start a 200,000-word book on Kaggle, pause anytime, and resume from the exact chunk on Android or Windows.
  • Final M4B Integrity Gate: Verifies duration, chapter markers, and embedded BSJ1 synchronized sentence transcripts on every finished audiobook.

How Google Account Access Is Used

When you sign in with Google, SpeakStudio uses Google OAuth 2.0 solely to manage your personal MyDrive/SpeakStudio/ audiobook workspace (manuscripts, narrator reference voice clips, resume checkpoints, and completed .m4b audiobooks) so your library stays synced across your Android phone, Windows PC, and personal cloud notebook runtime.

SpeakStudio operates client-side and does not run centralized servers that collect, inspect, or sell your Google Drive files. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for full details, including our Google API Services Limited Use compliance.
